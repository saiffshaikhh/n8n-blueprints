Weekly Rhythm
What Happens Every Week
Consistent schedule so nothing slips and your team always knows what to expect.
Monday

List Hygiene + Validation

Incoming lists run through validation before entering Instantly. Hard bounces suppressed. Unsubscribes synced. Prior campaign bounces excluded.

Tuesday

Campaign Setup + QA

New campaign loaded, sequences reviewed, sending schedule set, reply notifications tested to confirm they're routing to your team correctly.

Wednesday

Campaign Launch

Campaign goes live. First-hour delivery monitored for early bounce signals. Domain health confirmed before full send volume ramps.

Thu–Fri

Monitoring + Flags

Open rates, bounce rates, complaint signals, and inbox placement checked. Any issue flagged to your team with a recommended fix before it compounds.

Friday

Weekly Report

Campaign performance, deliverability health, domains added, issues resolved, and anything to change heading into next week. Sent by EOD Friday.
