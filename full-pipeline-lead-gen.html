How I run the pipeline
1
ICP deep-dive (the 80% that nobody does)
Every pipeline starts here. Firmographics, tech stack, hiring signals, funding triggers, role buyer committee, pain language. Wrong ICP = wasted outbound. I treat this as the foundation, not an afterthought.
2
Source 5,000+ qualified leads per week
Apollo for volume + intent filters, LinkedIn Sales Navigator for decision-maker precision, Apify for custom scrapers when a source doesn't have an API. Stack selection depends on ICP — there is no one-size-fits-all.
3
Match outbound to the audience
Founders respond to LinkedIn DMs. Ops managers respond to email. Clinic owners respond to WhatsApp. I map the channel-message-cadence combo to each ICP segment — that's where 100% of my conversion lift comes from.
4
Book meetings and protect the show rate
Qualification on the booking form, calendar holds, multi-channel reminders 24h and 1h before the call. Show rate is where most lead-gen operators lose — I optimize it as an explicit KPI.
5
Own the close, onboarding, and retention loop
Sales call playbooks, cycle compression tactics, onboarding for fast activation, churn-risk monitoring, and win-back sequences for dormant accounts. Lead gen that doesn't convert to retained revenue is vanity.
