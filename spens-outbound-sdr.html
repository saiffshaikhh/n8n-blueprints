Research Process
What I check before writing a single word
Every email opens with a genuine observation about that clinic specifically. No mail-merged compliments. Each audit takes 4 to 6 minutes and surfaces a real growth gap.
01
Website Speed + Mobile
Check PageSpeed, mobile layout, Core Web Vitals. Slow sites cost bookings. I note the exact issue to reference in the email.
02
Before/After Gallery Quality
Premium aesthetic clinics win or lose on visual credibility. I check gallery layout, photo quality, and whether results are showcased or buried.
03
Trust Signals
GMB reviews, testimonials, accreditations, doctor bios. Missing trust signals are a direct conversion leak I can point to.
04
Booking UX
How many clicks to book? Is there a form, a phone number, a chatbot? Friction in the booking flow is a quantifiable problem.
05
Local SEO Visibility
Search the clinic's top 3 services in their city. Are they on page one? If not, that is a revenue gap I can quantify in the email opener.
06
Decision Maker ID
Find the practice owner or marketing lead on LinkedIn or the About page. Every email goes to a named person, never "info@".

Follow-Up System
5-touch sequence, every prospect
Growth is in the follow-up. Every non-reply gets the full sequence. Every reply gets a manual response within the hour.
D1
Send
Touch 1 — Initial Send
Personalized audit email
Website gap identified during research forms the opener. Named decision maker. Under 100 words. Single soft CTA asking for 15 minutes.
D3
F/U
Touch 2 — Bump
One-line follow-up
Two sentences. Reference the original email. No repetition. Designed to resurface in a busy inbox without friction.
D7
Val
Touch 3 — Value Add
Relevant case study or stat
A short result from a similar clinic (same niche, same problem). Gives them a reason to engage beyond the original pitch.
D14
Alt
Touch 4 — Alternative Ask
Lower-commitment CTA
Instead of a call, offer a quick audit PDF or answer a specific question about their site. Lowers the barrier for skeptical decision makers.
D21
Close
Touch 5 — Break-Up
Polite close-out
Short note letting them know this is the last email. Break-up emails often get the highest reply rate of the sequence. Prospect moved to a re-engage list at 90 days.
