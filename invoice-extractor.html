Invoice Extractor — Gmail to Sheets

Every minute, this workflow scans your Gmail for unread emails with "Invoice" in the subject, uses GPT-4o to extract the invoice number, vendor, amount, currency, and due date — then appends a clean row to your Google Sheet. Zero manual data entry.

How it works
1
Watch the inbox
Gmail is polled every minute for unread emails matching subject:Invoice. Full email body, sender, and metadata are captured.
2
Extract with AI
GPT-4o reads the email and returns a structured object: invoice_number, vendor, amount, currency, due_date. Missing fields return as null.
3
Flatten + enrich
Extracted fields are combined with email context (subject, sender, received date) into a single flat row.
4
Log to Google Sheets
One row appended per invoice, columns auto-mapped — instant searchable audit trail ready for accounting.
