How the build runs end to end
1
Translate the ICP into mining filters
Titles, seniority, geos, company size, industry, tech triggers, exclusions. Built off your brief, not a generic template. The cleaner the filters, the higher the match rate downstream.
2
Mine across multiple sources, not one
Verified directories, professional networks, industry registries, and custom scrapes for any source without an open API. Multi-source coverage is the only way to hit volume without sacrificing precision.
3
Verify every record through a 4-step waterfall
Each step only runs on rows the previous one missed. Catch-alls and role-based addresses dropped, duplicates removed across all sources and historical sends. Bounce rate stays under 2%, end of story.
4
Sync into your CRM, not into a dead spreadsheet
Every verified lead lands in your CRM with source, signal, stage, and any custom properties you need for attribution. No imports to chase, no stale lists, no copy-paste handoffs.
5
Light up the dashboard so the data is usable
A single dashboard view: total leads, verified rate, synced count, source breakdown, pipeline stage funnel, conversion to booked calls. Stakeholders see one screen, not five tabs. Updated live as the CRM updates.
6
Hand off the system, not just the list
Documented filters, the verification logic, the CRM mapping, the dashboard configuration. Your team can run the same system on the next ICP without me. The deliverable is the system, not a one-time data dump.
