How I run the engagement
1
Audit the current stack and pipeline
First week: I review your Apollo filters, Smartlead/Reply.io sequences, HubSpot pipeline structure, channel mix, and reply rates. Output is a 1-pager — what's working, what's leaking, what to kill, what to add. No tool changes until we agree on the diagnosis.
2
Match channel-message-cadence to each ICP segment
Founders respond to LinkedIn DMs. Ops managers respond to email. SaaS revenue leaders respond to WhatsApp follow-ups. I map the channel + opener style + cadence per segment, not a single sequence for everyone. This is where most outbound fails — generic cadence on the wrong channel.
3
Build sequences with deliverability as a hard constraint
Smartlead + Reply.io for email — mailbox rotation, warmup respected, send caps tuned to your domain age, spam-trigger words filtered. WhatsApp via compliant BSP (Wati / 360dialog). LinkedIn at safe daily limits. Deliverability is a KPI, not an assumption.
4
Wire HubSpot as the single source of truth
Every reply, meeting booked, no-show, and stage change writes back to HubSpot via API. Custom properties for source channel, sequence touchpoint, signal that triggered outreach. Sales sees one timeline; you see attribution clean enough to know what to scale.
5
Automate the boring parts, leave humans for the close
Reply triage, lead qualification, calendar booking, no-show recovery, dormant-account reactivation — all automated via n8n + Claude. AEs see only conversations worth their time. Saves 10–15 hours/week per rep.
6
Weekly reporting + strategic recommendations
Friday digest: what shipped, what each channel produced, top-performing variants, what I'd test next week. I don't just operate — I advise on stack, sequence experiments, and channel mix as the program scales.
