How I run a campaign build
1
Ingest your ICP + signals into a Clay table
You give me the ICP doc and signal list. I translate them into Clay people/company finder filters — title regex, headcount bands, funding stage, geo, exclusions. Output: a clean source table sized to your weekly send target.
2
Layer the signal columns
Hiring signals (BuiltWith / job board scrape), funding (Crunchbase / news), tech stack (BuiltWith / Wappalyzer), intent (G2 / Bombora if available), recent triggers (LinkedIn posts, press). Each signal becomes a Clay column you can filter and rank on.
3
Wire the enrichment waterfall
Apollo → Hunter → Snov → Clearbit/Datagma → Findymail, then ZeroBounce on top. Conditional logic: each step only fires on rows still missing a verified email. Tuned for your geo mix (US-heavy stacks differ from EU-heavy).
4
Build the personalization columns
Website scrape → AI summary, recent LinkedIn post → relevance line, signal-based opener, custom variables for your copy ({{first_line}}, {{trigger}}, {{ice_breaker}}). Tied to your existing copy template so I'm not rewriting your voice.
5
Validate, dedupe, push to Instantly
MX + catch-all check, risk score filter, dedupe across both campaigns + your historical sends, then push to Instantly via API or formatted CSV. Mailbox rotation + send-cap aware. You get a Loom walking through the table before launch.
6
Hand off + iterate
Documented Clay table (so anyone on your team can clone it), credit usage report, list of any rows that failed enrichment with reason, and a 1-pager on what to A/B for round 2. Ongoing builds drop to 1-day turnaround once your stack is templated.
