The framework I write to
Eight rules borrowed from direct response. Every one of the 10 templates passes this checklist before it ships.
RULE 01
Subject under 5 words
Lowercase, casual, never ALL CAPS or question-marky. Reads like a real person sent it, not a marketing department.
RULE 02
P2P "I" voice
First person singular, never "we". A real human writing to a real human, not a brand broadcasting to a list.
RULE 03
One specific value prop with a number
"2,000 verified contacts in 72 hours" beats "we deliver high quality leads". Numbers anchor belief, vague language kills it.
RULE 04
Risk reversal in every offer
Refund clause, money-back guarantee, free trial, or "if X doesn't happen, you don't pay". Makes "no" harder than "yes".
RULE 05
One CTA, one ask
Never "book a call AND download AND reply". One clear next step per email, with a specific time anchor where possible.
RULE 06
Body under 80 words for cold, under 150 for warm
Mobile-first reading. If it doesn't fit on a phone screen without scrolling, it gets cut.
RULE 07
No corporate filler
No "leverage", no "seamlessly", no "world class", no "I hope this finds you well". Reads like a peer, not a vendor.
RULE 08
Reading level grade 6 to 8
Short sentences, plain words, scannable on a phone in 8 seconds. Complexity loses opens, not converts.

5
Case study drop
Warm
A real customer story with named numbers. Before, what changed, after. Anchors the reader's belief that the outcome is real for someone like them.
6

Convert
The pitch. Problem, solution, proof, risk reversal, CTA. Built to convert warm readers without burning trust.
7
Urgency or last-call email
Convert
Real urgency, not invented urgency. Closing date, last seats, price change. Honest scarcity converts. Fake scarcity erodes the list.
8
Re-engagement and win-back
Retain
Sent to dormant subscribers. One sentence acknowledging the gap, one new specific value prop, one soft CTA. No begging.

Newsletter or update
Retain
A repeatable format your team can fill in weekly or monthly. One headline insight, one resource, one ask. Designed for skim reading on a phone.
10
Referral or advocacy ask
Retain
Sent to happy customers. Specific ask, low friction, makes the customer the hero. The cheapest source of new revenue your business has.
