Show Rate: 54% → 75%+
No-Show Recovery Sequence
Multi-touch, channel-aware, micro-commitment driven. Not a reminder blast. A commitment architecture.
T − 48h
SMS + WhatsApp

Confirmation + Calendar Hold

Appointment confirmation with .ics calendar link. "Reply C to confirm" micro-action. Sets the first commitment anchor and reduces cognitive friction to show.

T − 24h
Form + WhatsApp

Micro-Commitment Form

3-question intake: case type, urgency, top concern. Framed as "helps us prepare for YOUR consultation." Increases psychological investment and reduces no-show rate. Non-responders get a WhatsApp nudge at T-20h.

T − 4h
Email + WhatsApp

Value Delivery Video

"What to expect in your free immigration consultation" — branded Loom or video. Delivers real value before the call, raises the perceived cost of missing the appointment.

T − 1h
Channel-Aware

Personalized Reminder

WhatsApp for WhatsApp-origin leads. SMS for form leads. Email for all. First-name personalized. Easy one-tap reschedule link included. No generic blasts.

No-Show
Rescue Sequence

Immediate Recovery

Within 15 min: "We held your spot — can we reschedule?" with one-tap booking link. T+2h: call attempt from closer. T+24h: "We understand things come up" reactivation message.

T + 3d
Revival or Disposition

Final Attempts + Bucket Move

Two more touch attempts across channels. After 3 failed contacts: lead tagged and moved to Old Lead Revival bucket for quarterly reactivation campaigns.

Behavior-Based Post-Consultation Paths
Consultation outcome is tagged immediately after the closer call. Each scenario fires a different sequence — not one generic drip.
🤔 Needs to Think
  • D+1: Client outcome story — social proof email
  • D+3: "Questions we usually get at this stage"
  • D+5: Testimonial video via WhatsApp
  • D+8: Urgency frame — deadlines, consequences of delay
  • D+14: Soft close from assigned attorney
  • D+21: Move to cold revival bucket
💰 Price Objection
  • D+1: Payment plan options + total case value framing
  • D+3: ROI breakdown — cost of inaction vs. hiring
  • D+5: "What's included" breakdown + comparisons
  • D+7: Final payment plan offer with deadline
  • D+10: Last-attempt call from closer
  • D+14: Move to cold revival
📄 Missing Documents
  • Same-day: Document checklist emailed + WhatsApped
  • D+3: "Do you have X yet?" check-in SMS
  • D+7: Attorney follow-up call attempt
  • D+14: Document collection reminder + deadline
  • D+21: Case stall escalation sequence
  • D+30: Handoff to legal team with status note
👻 Ghosted
  • D+1: SMS check-in (different channel from consultation)
  • D+3: WhatsApp voice note from closer
  • D+7: "Last attempt" email with new angle
  • D+14: Move to Old Lead Revival bucket
  • D+90: Quarterly reactivation campaign blast
  • D+180: Annual re-engagement if still no response
