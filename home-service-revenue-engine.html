The Framework
8-System Revenue Engine
Every system is a self-contained GHL module. Each one runs independently and talks to the others via tags and pipeline stage changes.
System 01
⚡
Instant Lead Response
First contact fires in under 60 seconds from any lead source — form, Facebook Lead Ad, Google LSA, chat widget, or inbound call.
Trigger: Lead created (any source)
Action: SMS + email within 60s
Target: < 60s
System 02
📞
Missed Call Text-Back
Missed call fires an instant SMS: "We just missed you — want to book a quick estimate?" Catches the lead before they call the next contractor.
Trigger: Missed inbound call
Action: SMS within 30s
Target: < 30s
System 03
🔁
Follow-Up Sequences
5-touch sequence over 14 days. SMS, email, and voicemail drop. Stops automatically when lead books or replies. No spam, no chaos.
Trigger: Lead unresponsive after first contact
Action: D1, D3, D7, D10, D14
14-day window
System 04
📅
Booking Automation
GHL calendar link delivered via SMS. Booking confirmation fires instantly. Reminder sent 24h and 1h before appointment. No-show re-engagement built in.
Trigger: Calendar link clicked → Appointment booked
Action: Confirm + remind
Auto reminders
System 05
⭐
Review + Reputation
Job marked Done in pipeline triggers a review request SMS with direct Google link. 2-day follow-up if no review. Stops when review is left.
Trigger: Stage moved to "Job Done"
Action: SMS review request → Google
Post-job auto
System 06
📊
Pipeline + Dashboard
Clean 6-stage pipeline for home service. Every lead has a stage, a source, and a status. Owner opens GHL and sees exactly where every deal lives.
Stages: New → Contacted → Appt Set → Job Done → Review → Closed
Tags: Standardized taxonomy
Always visible
System 07
🤖
AI SMS Response Layer
Simple AI handles the top 5 lead questions: availability, service area, pricing range, what to expect, and booking link. Controlled. No hallucinations. Hands off when uncertain.
Trigger: Inbound SMS from new lead
Action: GPT classifies + responds or escalates
Controlled AI
System 08
💬
Chat to SMS Capture
GHL chat widget on client website. Visitor asks a question — GHL captures name and phone, moves conversation to SMS. Lead is in the CRM before they leave the site.
Trigger: Chat widget interaction → phone captured
Action: Contact created + Speed-to-Lead fires
Instant capture
Full Lead Lifecycle

Simplicity First
What Is In vs What Is Out
The fastest way to kill a system that needs to run 24/7 for 50+ clients is to over-engineer it. Here's the line.
IN — What Gets Built OUT — What Gets Left Out
One clean follow-up sequence (5 touches, hard stop) Nested conditional branches for every lead scenario
AI handles 5 FAQs, escalates everything else AI tries to handle full sales conversations
6 clear pipeline stages every team member understands 14-stage pipelines with ambiguous transitions
Tags: source, status, opt-out, reviewed (4 categories) 50+ tags no one can maintain
Workflow stops when lead replies or books Workflows that keep running regardless of lead behavior
One master snapshot, 5 client variables Custom builds per client
Review request fires once, one follow-up if no response Aggressive review drips that get complaints

  • Day 4: System 03 — 5-touch follow-up sequence

Reputation, AI + Snapshot
Complete the full 8-system framework, harden the logic, document everything, and cut the client-ready snapshot.
  • Day 6: System 05 — Review request + follow-up
  • Day 7: System 07 — AI SMS layer (controlled FAQ responses)
  • Day 8: System 08 — Chat widget to SMS capture
  • Day 9: Full integration test — lead in, all 8 systems fire correctly
  • Day 10: Master snapshot taken, Loom walkthroughs recorded
  • Day 10: Client onboarding checklist and maintenance guide delivered
