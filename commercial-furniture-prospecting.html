How it works
1
Capture leads from every source
Website forms, paid ads, LinkedIn Sales Nav, Apollo exports, and targeted scrapes of office-relocation and new-build permit data all funnel into one pipe — no manual CSV imports.
2
Qualify with Claude against your ICP
Claude reads your knowledge base — ICP, product catalog, pricing tiers, past deals — enriches each lead with firmographics (headcount, industry, recent funding, office moves), and scores fit. Junk gets dropped; hot leads get flagged.
3
Store clean, enriched records
Qualified leads land in the central database with full context — contact, company, fit score, reasoning, and recommended next touch. Ready for outreach with zero data cleanup.
4
Run multi-channel outreach
Personalized sequences fire across Slack, SMS, email, and cold calls — messaging tuned to the prospect's role, industry, and trigger event. Inbound replies route straight to the right rep.
5
Sync back to your CRM
Every touch — sent, opened, replied, booked — logs into HubSpot or Salesforce automatically. The loop closes: CRM signals feed back into Claude so the next outreach wave gets smarter.
