What ships in Week 1

Acquisition Engine

  • Multi-source lead capture (forms, ads, scraping, LinkedIn)
  • AI-powered lead scoring against your ICP
  • Enrichment waterfall (verify to under 2% bounce)
  • Automated channel routing based on lead score
  • Duplicate detection and merge logic

CRM + Reporting

  • 5-stage pipeline with automated transitions
  • Follow-up task generation (calls, emails, touches)
  • Live dashboard: source, conversion, pipeline value
  • Weekly digest reports to management
  • Feedback loop: CRM data retrains scoring model
Rollout
30 / 60 / 90 day plan
Days 1 to 30
Foundation
Audit existing processes. Define ICP and scoring criteria. Set up CRM pipeline stages. Deploy lead capture from top 2 channels. First leads flowing within 4 days.
Days 31 to 60
Scale
Activate all outreach channels. A/B test messaging. Launch live dashboard. Automate follow-up sequences. Optimize conversion at each pipeline stage.
Days 61 to 90
Optimize
Retrain scoring model with real conversion data. Kill underperforming channels. Double down on winners. Hand off SOPs to your team for long-term operation.
