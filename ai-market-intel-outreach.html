What ships every week
The two tracks share one AI brain but produce different artifacts. Stakeholders see both flowing on a predictable cadence.
Track A · Intelligence

Decision papers and market scans

  • 1 to 2 page decision paper per question, structured (situation, options, risk, recommendation)
  • Competitor pricing and positioning scans via Perplexity plus deep research with Claude
  • TAM, SAM, SOM sizing using public data plus AI-assisted triangulation
  • Feasibility memos for new ventures or product bets
  • Source-cited so stakeholders can audit any claim
Track B · Outreach

Expandi LinkedIn and Mailchimp campaigns

  • Expandi sequences with safe daily limits, smart inbox, A/B variants per ICP segment
  • Mailchimp campaigns (broadcast plus drip) with segmented audiences and tagged conversions
  • Copy written tight to the segment, not generic templates
  • Weekly performance digest: open, reply, accept, click, conversion
  • Optimization loop, not set-and-forget
How I run the role
1
Translate every brief into a structured AI workflow
A strategic question becomes a research plan with sources, a campaign request becomes an ICP and sequence brief. Same operating discipline, different artifacts. Nothing goes into the AI without a defined output and a quality bar.
2
Use the right AI for the right job
Perplexity for live web grounding and source-cited research. Claude for long-form synthesis, decision papers, and reasoning under ambiguity. ChatGPT for fast iteration on copy and creative variants. Outputs always cross-checked, never single-source.
3
Run Expandi within safe limits, optimize on real data
Conservative daily caps so no LinkedIn account flags. Connection messages, inMails, and follow-ups segmented per ICP. Weekly check on accept rate and reply rate. Variant that beats the control gets promoted, the loser gets killed.
4
Use Mailchimp for the lifecycle, not just blasts
Audience segmentation by source, lifecycle stage, and engagement level. Broadcast for announcements, automation flows for nurture and reactivation, transactional templates for onboarding. Conversion tracking back into the segment so reporting is honest.
5
Close the loop weekly
One Friday digest covers both tracks: decision papers shipped, campaigns running, top variants, conversion metrics, and a short list of what I would test or research next week. Stakeholders read one document, not five tools.
