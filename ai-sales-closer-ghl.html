Structured Sales Process
Lead to Close — 6 Steps
Every call follows this structure. Nothing left to guessing or improvisation.
01

Discovery + Rapport

Understand their business, current tech stack, and daily pain points. Speak their language. Let them talk 70% of the first 5 minutes.

02

Pain Identification

Get specific: how many leads are they losing? How many hours on manual tasks? What would change if those hours came back? Quantify the cost of inaction.

03

AI Solution Demo

Show what the AI automation does for their specific case. Not a generic pitch deck. A walkthrough of what their business looks like with the system running.

04

ROI Framing

Translate the solution into dollars. If the system saves 20 hours a week at $50/hr, that's $4,000/month. The $5K build pays for itself in 5 weeks.

05

Objection Handling

Address price, timing, trust, and technical concerns using specific frameworks (see below). No defensiveness. Pure problem-solving posture.

06

Close + Onboarding Handoff

Direct close: "Want to get started this week?" Collect payment. Move to onboarding pipeline in GHL. Intro to implementation team same day.

Objection Handling
The 5 Objections You'll Hear on Every Call
Every objection is a question in disguise. Here's how each one gets reframed.
"Too expensive"
Reframe to cost of inaction. "You mentioned you're losing 15 leads a month to slow follow-up. At your average deal size that's $X/month walking out the door. The system pays for itself in [weeks]."
"Need to think"
Isolate the real concern. "Totally fair. What specifically do you want to think through? Is it the investment, the timeline, or whether this actually works for your business?" Then address the real one.
"Bad timing"
Flip to urgency. "When is good timing to stop losing $X a month? The system takes 2 weeks to build. You're losing leads every day between now and when it's live."
"I don't trust AI"
Ground it in specifics. "It's not replacing you or your team. It's handling the repetitive stuff — follow-ups, booking, reminders — so your team only talks to people ready to buy. You stay in control."
"Send me info"
Micro-commitment close. "Happy to. Let me send you the system walkthrough. If it makes sense after you watch it, are you open to a 10-minute call Thursday to answer any questions?" Book the next step before you hang up.
