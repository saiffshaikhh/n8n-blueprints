Outbound Game Plan, distilled from one meeting into a system that ships.

Convert a 15,000-lead database into closed customers for the trading algorithm license. Personalized AI cold email at scale, lukewarm leads routed to setters via a custom multi-pronged dialer, the full motion wired into the CRM.

01
Objective

Convert a 15,000-lead database into closed customers for our trading algorithm license by warming leads via personalized AI emails at scale, then routing interested leads to setters for qualification calls via our custom multi-pronged dialer.

Primary touchpoint · Cold email (personalized at scale with AI)
Qualification · Outbound dialer powered by SignalWire
Subsidiary touchpoint · SMS / iMessage (scope and weight TBD)
02
ICP and Product

Product. Trading algorithm sold as a license.

ICP. Multiple defined cohorts (existing, not re-scoped in this meeting).

Volume targets. Deferred until budget and tooling allocation is finalized.

05
Sequence Design

A deliberately short cadence. The goal is to surface intent, not to nurture indefinitely.

Email 1
Personalized intro
AI-personalized based on the data points we have on the lead. Pitches how the trading algorithm helps them specifically.
CTA. "If this sounds like something you'd be interested in, just say yes and we'll give you a ring at [phone]." Phone field pulled from the database where available.
Email 2
Value-prop follow-up
Reinforces the value proposition for non-responders.
Closes the loop on the opt-in ask. No further chase steps in v1.
That's the full sequence. No further chase steps in v1.
06
The "Yes" Trigger, CRM Workflow

When a lead replies with interest:

i
Reply parsed
Reply content is parsed by the CRM workflow.
ii
Setter notified
Setter is notified inside the CRM.
iii
Hot Lead surfaced
Lead queued in the Conversations page and surfaces in the Hot Lead view.
iv
Setter calls
Setter calls via the dialer for the qualification conversation.
Instantly reply events also flow into the Conversations section so the full thread is visible alongside any other channel touchpoints.

08
Disqualified Leads, Downsell Workflow
↻
Disqualified leads are not discarded. A separate downsell email workflow will be built to offer a secondary product to leads who don't qualify for the primary algorithm license. Spec for this workflow is a follow-up item.
09
Ideal Lead Journey
An ICP lead receives a personalized email → expresses interest → setter calls and qualifies → lead books a call with a closer → closer closes → lead becomes a customer.
Every design decision below should be evaluated against whether it improves the throughput or quality of this single path.
10
Quality Targets

Guiding principles for the next phase. The motion must maximize:

Lead quality

Only ICP-fit leads enter the funnel.

Email deliverability

Domain and mailbox infra and warming protect inboxing.

Email copy quality

AI personalization is only as good as the data it's grounded in.

Phone-data quality

Setters cannot waste cycles on bad numbers.

11
Reporting

Outbound funnel reporting will live in the CRM Roster, with a dedicated outbound-email reporting view to be built in a later phase.
