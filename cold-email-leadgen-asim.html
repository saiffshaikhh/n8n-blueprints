How I'd run it for you
1
Build the targeted list, not just scrape
Apollo for UK and EU firmographic filters, LinkedIn Sales Nav for decision-maker precision, Apify for any directory or niche source without an API. Filters tuned to your ICP, exclusions enforced (no competitors, no bad-fit segments, no people you've already pitched).
2
Verify every email through a 4-step waterfall
Hunter, Snov, Findymail, ZeroBounce in cascade. Catch-all and role-based addresses dropped. Bounce rate stays under 2%, which keeps the sender domain healthy and inbox placement above 90%.
3
Write copy that earns the reply
Tight subject lines under 5 words. P2P opener, one specific value prop with a number, soft CTA built around a 15 minute call. Sequences split A and B from day one so we know within 500 sends which variant wins.
4
Run a healthy sending stack
8 to 12 mailboxes across 3+ root domains. Full SPF, DKIM, DMARC. Two-week warmup before any cold sends. 25 to 35 sends per mailbox per day max. Smart sending on. Time zone matched to UK and US prospect inboxes.
5
Wire the CRM as a single source of truth
HubSpot, Pipedrive, or Close (whichever you run) gets every reply, every booked call, every stage change synced via API. Custom properties for source, sequence, and signal so attribution stays clean. You see one timeline, not five tools.
6
Report Friday, optimize Monday
Friday digest: emails sent, opens, replies (positive, neutral, negative), calls booked, deals progressed, top-performing variant, what to test next. No mystery, no vanity metrics, just the funnel math you can act on.
