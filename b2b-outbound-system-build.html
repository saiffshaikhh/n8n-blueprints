Cold Outbound
5-Touch 21-Day Cold Email Sequence
Short, human, pain-first copy. Each touch uses a different angle. No "just following up" language.
Day 1 — Touch 1 — Pattern Interrupt
Subject: quick q re [company] outbound
"[First name] — noticed [specific signal about their role or company]. Most [title]s I talk to hit a wall around [core pain point]. Worth 10 min to talk through it?"
Personalized opener Pain-first Single CTA
Day 4 — Touch 2 — Social Proof
Subject: what worked for [similar company]
"Didn't hear back. Figured I'd share one thing that moved the needle for [company in same space] — not a pitch, just a data point you might find useful."
Social proof Value-first
Day 8 — Touch 3 — Outcome Reframe
Subject: [specific result] in [timeframe]?
"[First name] — if [specific outcome] was handled in the next 30 days, what does that change for Q[X]? Genuinely asking."
Outcome reframe Question only
Day 14 — Touch 4 — Resource Offer
Subject: something that might help
"Built a quick [blueprint / doc / breakdown] around the [pain] problem most [title]s deal with. Happy to send it over — no strings."
Low friction offer Value delivery
Day 21 — Touch 5 — Breakup
Subject: closing the loop
"Going to stop reaching out after this one. If timing changes, you know where to find me — [first name]."
Breakup email Creates urgency

LinkedIn
4-Touch LinkedIn DM Sequence
Expandi configured with your ICP. Connection request plus three follow-up DMs loaded and ready.
1
Day 0 — Connection Request (300 char note)
Personalized opener referencing their content or role
"[First name] — saw your post on [topic]. Working on something in the same space. Would love to connect."
2
Day 2 — First DM (fires on accept)
Thank you + growth signal observation
"Thanks for connecting. Noticed [company] is [signal — hiring, expanding, raising]. Curious — how are you handling [specific pain] as you scale?"
3
Day 5 — Value DM
Resource or insight drop — no ask
"Built a quick [framework / breakdown] on [pain topic] that's been getting traction with teams like yours. Happy to share — no strings."
4
Day 10 — Direct Ask
Clear CTA, low friction, time-specific
"[First name] — open to 15 min this week? Have something specific in mind for [company] I think is worth talking through."
