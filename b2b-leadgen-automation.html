Automation Flow
Four stages, zero manual steps
Stage 01 — Source
Automated lead capture
Apollo searches run on schedule against your ICP filters. New leads pulled, deduplicated against existing database, and queued for enrichment automatically.
Stage 02 — Qualify
AI scoring and routing
Claude scores each lead against ICP criteria. High-score leads go straight to personalized outreach. Mid-score leads enter a nurture track. Low-score leads get flagged for review.
Stage 03 — Outreach
Personalized sequences at scale
Emails generated with lead-specific personalization, pushed to Instantly or Smartlead via API. Follow-up touches fire automatically based on open and click behavior.
Stage 04 — Sync
CRM and reporting loop
Every reply, open, click, and meeting booked syncs to CRM in real time. Conversion data retrains the scoring model. Slack alert fires on every warm reply.
