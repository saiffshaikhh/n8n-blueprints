Audit — what we check day 1
1
DNS authentication
SPF, DKIM, DMARC correctly set per sending domain. Any misalignment = instant spam folder.
2
Domain strategy
Primary domain untouched? 3–5 secondary domains redirected to main site?
3
Warmup status
Instantly native warmup ON for 2–4 weeks, positive ramp curve, deliverability score ≥ 90%.
4
Per-mailbox volume
Staying under 30–50 sends/mailbox/day, or overblasting and getting filtered?
5
List hygiene & bounces
Pre-verified through Bouncer / MillionVerifier / Debounce? Bounce rate under 2%?
6
Copy audit
Plain text (no HTML bloat, no images, no pixels), <120 words, max 1 link, no spam triggers.
7
Personalization depth
Just {{firstName}} or real custom variables? Spintax on subject + opener?
8
Smart sending settings
Throttling on, randomized delays, sends mimic human pattern — not 9am-sharp blasts.
9
Inbox rotation
Campaign load balanced across all mailboxes, or stacking on one account?
10
Reply & unsubscribe handling
Unsubscribe tag present, negative replies auto-pause, positive replies routed out to CRM fast.
11
Reputation signals
Google Postmaster, Glock Apps, MXToolbox, Mail-Tester scores — tracked weekly?
Healthy Stack — what we install
1
Secondary-domain architecture
Never send from the main domain. 3–5 look-alikes × 2–3 mailboxes = 6–15 inboxes, 300–500 sends/day at safe volume.
2
Full DNS stack per domain
SPF + DKIM + DMARC (p=none first, tighten later) + custom tracking domain.
3
Warmup before outreach
2–4 weeks of Instantly warmup before the first campaign. Keep warmup ON even during active sending.
4
Volume discipline
30–50 new emails/mailbox/day, never above. Warmup volume on top.
5
Pre-campaign verification
Every list scrubbed before upload. Remove catch-all, risky, and role-based addresses.
6
Plain-text, short, 1-link copy
No images, no HTML styling, no tracking pixels, 40–120 words, conversational tone.
7
Real personalization + spintax
Custom vars beyond name (role, trigger, pain). Spintax on subject lines and first sentence.
8
Smart sending schedule
Throttled, 30s–3min randomized delays, business hours in recipient's timezone only.
9
Follow-up sequence design
3–4 touches, 2–3 day gaps, break threads on touch 3, kill sequence on reply or unsubscribe.
10
Reply routing
Positive replies → CRM within minutes. Negative → auto-blocklist. OOO → re-queue.
11
Weekly deliverability review
Instantly score + Google Postmaster + Glock Apps every Monday. Rotate out any mailbox below 90%.
