Healthy Practices
What Keeps Deliverability Above 94%
Good rates are not luck. They come from technical setup and discipline before a single email is sent.
✓
Email Validation Before Send
Full list run through ZeroBounce or NeverBounce. Remove hard bounces, catch-alls, role addresses, and spam traps. Expect 5–15% list shrink on a purchased list.
✓
DKIM, SPF, and DMARC Configured
All three DNS records set on sending domains before launch. Missing any one of these is the fastest way to hit spam folders at scale.
✓
Dedicated Sending Domains and IPs
Campaign runs on domains separate from your primary business domain. Reputation risk stays isolated. Shared IP pools are avoided.
✓
Complaint Rate Monitoring (Under 0.1%)
Google Postmaster Tools and Microsoft SNDS connected. If complaint rate hits 0.08%, the send pauses for review before continuing.
✓
ISP-Aware Throttling
Gmail, Outlook, Yahoo, and Apple Mail each have different rate limits. Sends are throttled per MX domain to avoid temporary deferrals at volume.
✓
Immediate Bounce and Unsub Processing
Hard bounces suppressed after first occurrence. Unsubscribes honored within 10 business days (CAN-SPAM) but processed in real time in practice.
