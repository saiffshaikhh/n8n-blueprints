How I'd run the operation
1
Inhale every offer in week one
Sit down with each client offer — what they sell, who they sell to, the language buyers use, the objections, the proof. No outbound goes out until I can pitch each offer better than a junior closer. This is the gap that breaks most outsourced cold-email teams.
2
Stand up the multi-tenant sending stack
Per offer: dedicated domains, mailbox count sized to volume (typically 15–30 per offer at this scale), warmup-aware send caps, IP/domain rotation. Inbox placement audited weekly with seed tests — not vibes. Bounce rate held under 2%, spam under 0.1%.
3
Clay + Claude Code = personalization at volume
Clay handles the waterfall enrichment and signal hunting. Claude Code via MCPs reads the enriched row, picks the angle, writes the first line, and chooses which sequence variant to drop the lead into. Personalization stays human-grade at 100K+ sends/week without a copywriter touching every row.
4
A weekly test → scale → kill loop
Every Monday I review per-offer dashboards: open rate, reply rate, positive reply %, booked-call %, cost per booked call. Winners get more volume. Losers get a hypothesis-driven rewrite or get killed. Nothing keeps running on autopilot just because it's "fine" — that's how teams plateau.
5
Reply handling that doesn't drop balls
Positive replies route to the closer's CRM with full context inside 5 minutes. Objections get categorized and fed back into copy iteration. OOO/auto-replies get parked for re-engagement. Nothing rots in an inbox.
6
Build new things, not just run the existing
New angles, new ICP slices, new channels (LI primer, voicemail drop), MCP tooling that compresses what currently takes the team hours into minutes. The role isn't "operate the playbook" — it's "evolve the playbook every week."
What I take ownership of on day 1
Inbox health & deliverability
Domains, DNS (SPF/DKIM/DMARC), warmup, blacklist monitoring, send caps, weekly placement audits.
List building & enrichment
Clay tables per offer, waterfall config, signal triggers, validation, suppression hygiene.
Copy & sequence performance
A/B variants, weekly review, hypothesis-driven rewrites, kill criteria for losers.
MCP & automation tooling
Claude Code routines that automate enrichment, reply triage, performance reporting, and copy generation.
Reporting to leadership
Weekly digest per offer: volume sent, positive replies, booked calls, $/booked call, what we're testing next.
Scaling decisions
When to add mailboxes, spin up new domains, expand to a new ICP slice, or pause volume to protect sender rep.
