GHL Workflow Builder
Automation Steps — Full Logic
This is the exact workflow configured in GHL. Triggers, actions, waits, and branching are all automated — zero manual steps.
⚡
Trigger

Form Submitted

Fires when Wholesale Intake Form is submitted. Contact is created with all vehicle fields populated.

📋
Action

Move to "Info Collected" Stage

Contact moves to Stage 2. Anti-loop tag added: wf-intake-received.

🤖
Action — OpenAI GPT

Send to GPT Pricing Logic

Webhook fires to n8n. Vehicle fields passed to GPT with the pricing prompt. GPT returns JSON: {"start": 150, "target": 250, "max": 300}.

💾
Action

Update Contact Custom Fields

ai_price_start, ai_price_target, ai_price_max written to contact record. Available in merge tags for SMS templates.

📱
Action — Send SMS

Send Approval Text to Owner

GHL sends SMS to owner's number using template with merged vehicle and price fields. Moves contact to "Awaiting Approval."

⏱
Wait

Wait for Owner Reply — Max 10 Minutes

GHL listens for inbound SMS reply on owner line. If reply received, branch fires immediately. If no reply in 10 min, auto-send branch fires.

🔀
Branch — If/Else

Owner Reply Check

Three paths based on reply content.

Reply = "YES"
Send customer: AI target price
Move to Approved/Sent
Reply = number (e.g. 225)
Parse number, send customer: override price
Update field + move stage
No reply in 10 min
Auto-send: AI target price
Log auto-send tag on contact
✅
Action

Contact Moves to "Approved / Sent"

Sent price logged to custom field. Conversation assigned to sales rep. Follow-up sequence begins 24h later if no response from customer.
