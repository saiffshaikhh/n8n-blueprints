10 reply intents, mapped to handling path
Auto means safe to send when confidence clears the threshold. Escalate goes to Slack first. Danger ignores confidence and pings you directly, every time.
Auto
Positive interest
"Yes tell me more" or similar. Drafter offers a 15 min slot, books on Calendly, syncs CRM.
Auto
Information request
"What does it cost / how does it work". Pulls answer from tenant KB, no fabrication.
Auto
Time shift
"Not now, ping me in Q3". Re-queues the lead for the date stated, polite acknowledgement.
Auto
Out of office
Parses return date, re-queues for that date plus 1, no follow-up wasted in the gap.
Auto
Wrong contact
"Talk to my COO". Asks politely for the name, scrapes a verified email, restarts sequence on the right person.
Escalate
Soft objection
"We already use X" or "We tried this before". Drafter writes a reframe, human approves before send.
Escalate
Pricing pushback
"Too expensive". Drafted reply uses social proof from KB. Human approves any number named.
Escalate
Hard no
"Not interested, remove me". Drafter generates a polite acknowledgement, tags lead as do-not-contact in CRM, escalates if any push.
Danger
Unsubscribe + complaint
"How did you get my email" or threats. Hard intercept, no auto-send. CRM marks suppressed, ping to founder Slack.
Danger
Lawyer / regulator mention
Keywords like "GDPR", "lawyer", "spam complaint". Instant escalation, zero auto-send, audit log preserved.

Follow-up cadence on non-responders
Five-touch cadence after the original cold sequence. Each touch is shorter than the last and varies the angle so the agent never sounds like a single template on repeat.

Bump

New angle

Day 21

Day 35
Break-up
Polite close. Often pulls a final reply by acknowledgment alone.
