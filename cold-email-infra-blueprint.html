DNS Configuration — Record by Record
SPF Record
Sender Policy Framework
Authorizes which mail servers can send from your domain. Strict alignment required for DMARC pass.
Type: TXT Host: @ Value: v=spf1 include:_spf.google.com include:sendgrid.net ~all
DKIM Record
DomainKeys Identified Mail
Cryptographic signature on every outbound email. Mandatory per Google & Yahoo 2024+ bulk sender policy.
Type: CNAME Host: google._domainkey Value: google._domainkey.yourdomain.com (auto-generated by mail provider)
DMARC Record
Domain-based Message Auth
Tells receiving servers what to do with SPF/DKIM failures. Start p=none, escalate to quarantine after 2 weeks.
Type: TXT Host: _dmarc Value: v=DMARC1; p=quarantine; rua=mailto:dmarc@yourdomain.com; pct=100; adkim=s; aspf=s;
Custom Tracking Domain
Click & Open Tracking
Never use your sequencer's shared tracking domain. Own subdomain protects sender rep from shared blacklists.
Type: CNAME Host: track Value: tracking.instantly.ai (or sequencer-specific endpoint) Use: track.yourbrand.com
MX Records
Mail Exchange
Routes inbound mail. Must be active even on sending-only domains — dead MX triggers spam filters immediately.
Type: MX Priority: 1 Host: @ Value: aspmx.l.google.com (Google Workspace) mail.protection.outlook.com (Microsoft 365)
Forwarding Setup
Reply-to Routing
Forward alias replies to a central inbox. Keeps sending domains clean while all replies land in one place.
Alias: outreach@yourbrand-01.com → Forward: replies@yourbrand.com Set "Reply-To" header in sequencer to route responses into CRM / Slack.
