Hallucination guardrails (your non-negotiable)
The rules below are enforced at the system level, not at the prompt level. Prompt rules drift. System rules don't.
  • ✕
    No order number, tracking link, or carrier name appears in any reply unless it came from a successful Shopify Admin API call in the same request.
  • ✕
    No refund amount auto-sends without passing the $ threshold gate. Above threshold drafts go to Slack with order data attached.
  • ✕
    No restock date or product launch date ever appears in agent output. The drafter is system-prompted to defer and the post-processor strips date patterns matching restock context.
  • ✕
    No promises of expedited shipping, free product, or compensation without a human approve in Slack first.
  • ✕
    No reply touches a customer if the confidence score from the safety gate is below the threshold you set. It routes to Slack instead, every time.
  • ✕
    No keyword in the danger list ("lawyer", "chargeback", "FDA", "BBB", "sue", "refund my entire order", "press") gets auto-handled. Hard intercept, instant Slack ping.
