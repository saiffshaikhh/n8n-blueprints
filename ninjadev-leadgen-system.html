How I'd run the role
1
Audit the current pipeline before changing anything
Week one is a full read of your Apollo filters, LinkedIn Sales Nav lists, HubSpot pipeline structure, Rechat segments, and current outreach copy. Output is a 1-pager: what is working, what is leaking, what to kill, what to add. No tool changes until we agree on the diagnosis.
2
Build the intent signal layer
Tech founders: funding rounds, hiring signals, AI hiring, BuiltWith stack matches. Luxury real estate: new listings above your price band, agent activity in target zips, off-market triggers via Rechat. Each signal becomes a column on the lead and feeds the prioritization queue.
3
Match channel and message to ICP segment
Founders respond to LinkedIn DMs with a sharp first-line. Ops leads respond to cold email with proof attached. Luxury property stakeholders respond to high-context, business-casual outreach that respects their time. One sequence per segment, never a generic blast.
4
Wire HubSpot as the single source of truth
Every reply, meeting booked, and stage change syncs back through API. Custom properties for source channel, signal that triggered outreach, sequence variant, ICP segment. Clean attribution by week one. No messy data, no orphan contacts, no duplicate records.
5
Stand up the live KPI dashboard
Open rate, CTR, CPL, conversion to call. Channel split, ICP segment split, intent signal performance, weekly trendlines. Built in HubSpot Reports for the in-CRM view and a Looker or Metabase board for the executive read. Stakeholders see one screen, not five tabs.
6
Optimize on the data, weekly
Friday digest: what shipped, what each channel produced, top-performing variants, what to test next week. Variants beating the control get promoted. Losers get killed. The system improves week over week without me explaining it on a call.
7
Speak credibly to founders about AI
I run automations on Claude, GPT, and Perplexity in production every week. I can hold a real conversation about agentic workflows, retrieval-augmented generation, and where a model fits versus where a deterministic pipeline does. Not surface-level fluency, working knowledge.
