How I run partnership outreach
1
Define the partner ICP first
Before any list-building, I lock the partner profile: which industries serve a similar client base, what company size band, what geographies, and what signal indicates an active partnership program. Wrong ICP = wasted outreach.
2
Research with the right stack per market
Apollo for volume + intent across US and EU, LinkedIn Sales Navigator for precision targeting and APAC coverage, Apify for custom scrapers when a niche source isn't on either platform. Tool choice is per-segment, not one-size-fits-all.
3
Identify the right decision-makers
Owners and founders for boutique agencies, Directors of Partnerships / BD for mid-market, Heads of Strategic Alliances for enterprise. I map the role to the company stage so the message lands with someone who can actually say yes.
4
Verify every contact before outreach
Every email runs through a verifier (Bouncer / MillionVerifier) before it goes near a sender. LinkedIn URLs and websites are spot-checked. Bounce rate stays under 2%, sender reputation stays clean, follow-ups don't get filtered.
5
Run a structured multi-touch cadence
Email + LinkedIn working together: short opening message, value-led follow-up at 3 days, soft break-up at 7 days, LinkedIn touch in parallel. Every touch logged in Google Sheets or the client's CRM with status, date, and next action.
6
Book the call, hand it off clean
When a partner replies positively, I qualify on a short async exchange, book the call directly into the client's calendar, and hand over a one-page brief — partner background, role, mutual fit hypothesis, suggested talking points. The client walks into the call ready.
