Deliverability First
Everything checked before a single email sends
Most email problems start here. A clean technical foundation means your emails reach inboxes, not spam folders, before copy or design matters.
✓
SPF Record
Sending domain authorized. No unauthorized server can spoof your brand.
✓
DKIM Signing
Every email cryptographically signed. Inbox providers trust signed mail.
✓
DMARC Policy
Policy set and reports flowing. Quarantine or reject spoofed sends.
✓
List Hygiene
Unengaged contacts suppressed. Bounce and complaint rates minimized.
✓
Sending Warm-Up
New domains or IPs ramped gradually. Reputation built before bulk sends.
✓
Unsubscribe Flow
One-click unsubscribe wired. CAN-SPAM and GDPR compliant from day one.

Core Sequence
Welcome sequence: 5 emails, 14 days
Every new lead enters this sequence automatically on opt-in. Educates, builds trust, and moves toward a first event booking without manual work.
✉️
Email 1 — Immediate
Welcome + What to Expect
Warm, personal intro from the founder. Sets expectations. Delivers the lead magnet or promised resource. One soft CTA to explore the flagship program.
💡
Email 2 — Day 2
Your Most Valuable Leadership Insight
Short, high-value teaching email. One concept from the training methodology. Positions the founder as an authority before pitching anything.
🗣️
Email 3 — Day 5
Social Proof + Transformation Story
Client result or attendee testimonial told as a story. Specific outcome. Makes the prospect picture themselves in the same situation.
📅
Email 4 — Day 9
Upcoming Class or Event Announcement
Next class or event promoted directly. Date, format, what they will walk away with. Registration link with urgency framed around limited spots.
🎯
Email 5 — Day 14
Last Chance + Direct Ask
Final nudge for the event or a direct offer. One question reply CTA for those who are not yet ready to book. Routes to nurture sequence on no-click.
